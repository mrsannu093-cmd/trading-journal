☁️ Cloud Sync vs. Local Export Backups

How they differ: Your primary journal database lives in Supabase PostgreSQL so your Android phone and laptop always load the same live account records. Export backups (CSV / JSON) are offline snapshots stored on your device for spreadsheet analysis, personal archiving, or disaster recovery.

1. Export Trade History (CSV)

Compatible with Excel and Google Sheets. Exports all sessions and trades.

2. Export Full Journal Backup (JSON)

Complete structured backup of your profile currency, sessions, and trades.

📥 Import & Restore JSON Backup

Validates the JSON structure before importing. Arbitrary SQL files are strictly rejected. Existing sessions on the same date and existing trade sequence numbers (`T.N.`) are protected against duplicate overwrites.
